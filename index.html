Our Story

Where Every Dish is a Discovery

Born from a lifelong obsession with world travel, Uncharted was founded in 2018 by Chef Mara Osei and her partner, sommelier Devraj Pillai. After years of eating their way through 40+ countries, they wanted to create a single destination where guests could experience the world's most vibrant flavors without leaving the table.

Every dish on our menu tells a story — sourced from local farms, inspired by grandmothers' kitchens, and elevated with thoughtful technique. We believe great food is the most honest form of cultural exchange, and we cook with that respect at the forefront of everything we do.

Our mission: to introduce curious eaters to dishes and ingredients they've never encountered, while honoring the traditions they come from.
